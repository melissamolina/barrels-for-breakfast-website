THE HISTORY

Playa Garza, Costa Rica.

Barrels for Breakfast started about 17 years ago in a little cabin by the ocean in Playa Garza, Costa Rica.

I needed a surfboard bag and didn’t want another generic one. So I started making my own by hand, using what was around me — Costa Rican coffee sacks, old banners, leftover fabrics and materials that could be given another life.

Soon, surfers began bringing their boards to the cabin. I measured each board and built each bag around the surfer and how they traveled — from riding a bike to the beach to flying across the world.

That idea still guides Barrels for Breakfast today: create better surf gear, waste less, and give back to the ocean.

Pura Vida. 🌊🇨🇷
